**Where you stand**  
The phone you received is defective (cracked screen) and the seller (Amazon) has refused to take it back or refund you. Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 a seller on a marketplace must not refuse to take back or refund goods that are defective [1][3]. The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 also gives you the right to a refund, replacement or compensation for a defective good [2].

**What you can do**  
1. **Contact the grievance officer** of Amazon in writing (email or letter). Keep your order ID, invoice, delivery proof, photos of the cracked screen and any chat/email with Amazon. The grievance officer must acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month [1][3].  
2. If the grievance officer does not resolve it, you can **register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline (1915)**, which will forward your complaint to Amazon [4].  
3. If still unresolved, **file a complaint with the District Consumer Commission** (Mysuru) online via e-Jagriti. The commission will admit the complaint within 21 days and may order replacement or refund with interest [5].

Send a written complaint to Amazon’s grievance officer today, attaching the order details, invoice, delivery proof and photos of the cracked screen.

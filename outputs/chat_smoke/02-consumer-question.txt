- The platform must have a grievance officer who must acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month [1][2].

**What you can do**  
1. **Write to the platform’s grievance officer** (use the contact shown on Amazon’s site), stating the order ID, payment proof, delivery proof and photos of the cracked screen, and ask for a refund or replacement.

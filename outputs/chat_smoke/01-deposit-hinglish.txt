LawBuddy [advice]:
**Where you stand**  
Security deposit ka return aapke rental agreement par depend karta hai; agar landlord ne agreement ke mutabiq deposit wapas nahi kiya to wo contract ka breach hai aur aap compensation maang sakte hain (Indian Contract Act, 1872, s. 73)[1]. Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 aam tor par high-rent ya naye buildings par lagu nahi hota, isliye aapka case zyada tar contract law ke tahat aayega[4]. Deposit wapas karne ka koi statutory limit nahi hai; amount aur return date aapke likhit agreement mein likha hota hai[5].

**What you can do** (cheapest se leke formal tak)  
1. Landlord ko likhit demand (message/email) bhejein, ek clear date (jaise 7 din) tak deposit wapas karne ke liye, aur reply ya na-reply ka record rakhein[3].  
2. Agar koi response nahi milta, to registered post se legal notice bhejein jisme facts, amount (₹2 lakh) aur deadline ho, aur batayein ki aap kanooni karwai karenge[3].  
3. Landlord ki sahmati se District Legal Services Authority ke through Lok Adalat mein settlement ki koshish karein; yahan ka award final aur binding hota hai[3].  
4. Agar sab fail ho jaye, to civil suit file kar sakte hain; ₹2 lakh ke liye appropriate court aapke shehar ke district court ho sakta hai[3].  

**Next step**  
Abhi turant landlord ko ek written demand bhejein - “Please return my security deposit of ₹2 lakh by 7 days from today (e.g., 7 Oct). No response will lead to a legal notice.” Is demand ka screenshot/email aur landlord ka koi reply (ya na-reply) sambhal ke rakhein.  

*If you have any written rental agreement, payment receipts, or proof of how you paid the deposit, keep them - they will be useful later.*  

(Notice: contract breach ke liye suit file karne ki limitation 3 saal hai, isliye jaldi action lein[3].)

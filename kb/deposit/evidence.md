Collect these before you send a demand or a notice. Keep copies; give originals to no one.

- **Rental agreement**, including any renewal, and the clause on the deposit and notice period.
- **Proof that you paid the deposit**: bank transfer statement, UPI screenshot, cheque details or a signed receipt.
- **Proof of rent payments**, to show nothing is due.
- **Proof that you moved out on time**: your notice to vacate, the landlord's reply, the date you handed over keys.
- **Condition of the home**: photos or video on moving in and moving out, with dates.
- **All messages** with the landlord or agent about the deposit, repairs and deductions.
- **Bills you paid** (electricity, water, maintenance) up to the day you left.

If something is missing, say so in your notice instead of guessing; a notice with wrong facts weakens your case.

LawBuddy's sources contain no legal limit on the size of a security deposit for homes in Karnataka.

The Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 does not set a maximum deposit. Where the Act talks about "deposit", it means a tenant
depositing rent with the Controller when the landlord refuses to accept it (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, s. 17). The
Karnataka Rent (Amendment) Act, 2025 changed the Controller's powers and the offences and penalties under the Act
(for example, fines in place of imprisonment); it did not add a deposit limit (Karnataka Rent (Amendment) Act, 2025,
ss. 2 to 5).

Claims online of a "two-month deposit cap" in Karnataka do not come from these laws. If someone relies on a deposit
cap, ask them which law and section it comes from.

So the amount of the deposit, and when it must be returned, is whatever your rental agreement says.

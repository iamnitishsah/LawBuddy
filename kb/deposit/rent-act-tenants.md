For the smaller group of tenancies the Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 covers (low rent, older buildings, in listed areas):

- A tenancy must be by a written agreement (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, s. 4).
- A tenant who pays rent, other charges or an advance is entitled to a signed written receipt from the landlord
  (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, s. 16(2)).
- If the landlord refuses to accept rent, the tenant may deposit it with the Controller (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999,
  s. 17).
- If a tenant paid any sum to the landlord in breach of the Act, the Controller may order the landlord to refund
  it, or adjust it against rent, on an application made within one year of the payment (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999,
  s. 15).
- Controllers are officers appointed by the State Government to decide these matters (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999,
  s. 23).

Whether the Act applies to a particular home turns on its rent, age and location; a lawyer or the DLSA can confirm.

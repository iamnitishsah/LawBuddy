The Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 regulates rent and eviction, but only for some premises. Its main rules (Chapters I to
III and V to VIII) apply in the areas listed in its First Schedule: Part A covers the cities constituted under the
Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976 and a radius of three kilometres around them; Part B covers areas within
City Municipal Councils (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, s. 2(1) and First Schedule).

Even in those areas, the Act does not apply to (Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, s. 2(3)):
- premises whose deemed rent or standard rent is above Rs. 3,500 a month in a Part A area, or above Rs. 2,000 a
  month in any other area;
- premises constructed or substantially renovated in the last fifteen years, counted from completion;
- premises used for non-residential purposes, except small commercial premises of up to fourteen square metres;
- premises of the Central or State Government, a local authority, a religious or charitable institution or a Wakf,
  and buildings of co-operative societies.

In practice, most rented flats and houses in Bengaluru and other cities pay more than these limits, so the Act
usually does not apply. The tenancy is then governed by the rental agreement and general contract law.

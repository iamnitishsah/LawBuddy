Many websites and older documents still quote laws that have been repealed. Do not rely on them:

- The **Consumer Protection Act, 1986** was repealed by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (s. 107). Consumer
  complaints now go to District, State and National Consumer **Commissions** (earlier called Forums).
- The **Payment of Wages Act, 1936**, the **Minimum Wages Act, 1948**, the **Payment of Bonus Act, 1965** and the
  **Equal Remuneration Act, 1976** were repealed by the Code on Wages, 2019 (s. 69).
- The **Karnataka Rent Control Act, 1961** was replaced by the Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 (s. 70).
- The **Indian Penal Code (IPC)**, the **Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC)** and the **Indian Evidence Act** were
  replaced in 2024 by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and the Bharatiya Sakshya
  Adhiniyam. Criminal matters are outside LawBuddy's scope; see a lawyer.

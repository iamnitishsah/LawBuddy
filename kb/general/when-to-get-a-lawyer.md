LawBuddy covers three everyday problems in Karnataka: a security deposit not returned, a consumer refund or defect,
and unpaid salary or final dues. It gives legal information, not legal advice.

**Talk to a lawyer or your District Legal Services Authority (free if you qualify) when:**
- you have received a court summons, a court notice or a legal notice with a deadline;
- the other side has already filed a case against you, or a case is pending;
- the amount is large, or the matter involves property ownership, title or eviction proceedings;
- the employer has closed down, become insolvent, or you are a government employee with service rules;
- you are unsure which court or authority to approach, or a time limit may have passed.

**Not covered by LawBuddy:** criminal matters, police complaints and FIRs, arrest or bail, family matters (divorce,
custody, maintenance), property title disputes, cheque bounce cases, tax, immigration, and law outside Karnataka's
central and state laws listed in its sources. LawBuddy says so and points to a lawyer or the DLSA.

**In an emergency** (violence, threats to safety, someone in danger), call **112** first.

You are a consumer if you bought goods, or hired or used a service, for a price that was paid or promised, fully or
in part, including in instalments (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 2(7)). Someone who uses the goods or service
with the buyer's approval, such as a family member, also counts.

You are **not** a consumer if you bought the goods for resale or for a commercial purpose, or used the service for a
commercial purpose. Buying something to earn your own livelihood by self-employment is not a commercial purpose
(Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 2(7), Explanation (a)).

Online purchases count: buying goods or hiring services includes transactions made online or through electronic
means, teleshopping, direct selling or multi-level marketing (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 2(7),
Explanation (b)).

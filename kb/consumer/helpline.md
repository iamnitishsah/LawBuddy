The National Consumer Helpline (NCH), run by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India, helps resolve
consumer grievances with companies before a formal complaint.

- Toll-free: **1915** (8 AM to 8 PM) or 1800-11-4000
- WhatsApp: +91 88000 01915
- Online: consumerhelpline.gov.in → "Register Grievance"

Many large companies and platforms are "convergence partners" of the NCH, so a grievance registered there is sent to
the company directly. If the matter is not resolved, the next step is a complaint to the District Consumer
Commission on e-Jagriti.

Source: consumerhelpline.gov.in, checked 2026-11-21.

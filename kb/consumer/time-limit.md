A Consumer Commission will not admit a complaint unless it is filed within **two years** from the date the cause of
action arose (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 69(1)). The cause of action is usually when the defect appeared, the
service failed, or the refund was refused.

A late complaint can still be heard if you show sufficient cause for the delay, and the Commission records its
reasons for accepting it (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 69(2)). Do not rely on this: file within two years.

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 put duties on online platforms and sellers:

- **No refusal to take back or refund** when goods or services are defective, deficient or spurious, not as
  advertised or agreed, or delivered late (except late delivery due to force majeure). This applies to sellers on a
  marketplace (E-Commerce Rules, 2020, r. 6(3)) and to platforms that sell their own inventory (r. 7(4)).
- **Grievance officer.** Every platform must appoint a grievance officer and show their name and contact details on
  the platform (r. 4(4)). The officer must acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month
  of receiving it (r. 4(5)). Marketplace sellers must do the same (r. 6(4)(b)).
- **Refunds** accepted by the platform must be paid within a reasonable time, as prescribed by the Reserve Bank of
  India or other applicable law (r. 4(10)).
- **Cancellation charges** cannot be imposed on you unless the platform bears similar charges when it cancels an
  order itself (r. 4(8)).

Keep the order ID, invoice, delivery proof, photos or videos of the product, and every chat or email with customer
care. Write to the grievance officer in writing and note the date: if there is no answer within a month, you have
a clear record for the next step.

**Which Commission.** It depends on the value of the goods or services you paid for: up to Rs. 50 lakh goes to the
District Commission; above Rs. 50 lakh and up to Rs. 2 crore to the State Commission; above Rs. 2 crore to the
National Commission (Consumer Protection (Jurisdiction) Rules, 2021, rr. 3 to 5). Most everyday complaints go to the
District Commission.

**Which district.** You can file where the opposite party lives or does business or has a branch, where the cause of
action arose, or **where you live or work** (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 34(2)). So you can usually file in
your own district, even against a company based elsewhere.

**Who can file.** The consumer, a recognised consumer association, or several consumers with the same interest
together, with the Commission's permission (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 35(1)).

**How.** A complaint may be filed electronically (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 35(1), proviso), through the
e-Jagriti portal (e-jagriti.gov.in). It must be accompanied by the prescribed fee (s. 35(2)); check the current fee
on e-Jagriti for your claim amount.

You do not need a lawyer to file; you can present your own case.

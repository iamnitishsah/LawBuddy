- **Admission.** The District Commission should ordinarily decide within 21 days whether to admit the complaint. It
  cannot reject it without hearing you, and if it does not decide in time, the complaint is treated as admitted
  (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 36(2) and (3)).
- **Mediation.** If a settlement looks possible, the Commission may ask both sides to agree in writing, within five
  days, to mediation (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 37(1)).
- **Order.** If your complaint is proved, the Commission orders relief such as replacement, refund with interest,
  compensation and costs.
- **Appeal.** Either side can appeal a District Commission order to the State Commission within 45 days of the order
  (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s. 41).
- **If the trader ignores the order.** Not complying with a Commission's order is punishable with imprisonment from
  one month up to three years, or a fine from Rs. 25,000 up to Rs. 1 lakh, or both (Consumer Protection Act, 2019,
  s. 72(1)).

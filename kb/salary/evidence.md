- **Proof you were employed**: offer or appointment letter, ID card, joining email, PF/ESI number, or messages from
  your manager.
- **Proof of salary**: payslips, bank statements showing earlier salary credits, or the salary stated in the offer.
- **Proof of work in the unpaid period**: attendance records, timesheets, emails or work you submitted.
- **Exit documents** if you left: resignation email, acceptance, relieving letter, last working day.
- **Every message** asking for payment, and any promise the employer made to pay.
- **Who the employer is**: the company or proprietor's legal name and address, from the offer letter, payslip or
  GST/website details. You need the correct name for a notice or claim.

Write down the months unpaid and the exact amount for each month; LawBuddy will put them in a table.

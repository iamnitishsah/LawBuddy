The government appoints authorities (officers not below the rank of Gazetted Officer) to hear and decide claims under
the Code (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 45(1)). In Karnataka, ask the Labour Department which officer hears these
claims for your area.

- **Who can file:** you, a registered trade union you belong to, or an Inspector-cum-Facilitator. One application
  can cover many employees of the same establishment (s. 45(4) and (5)).
- **Time limit:** within **three years** from when the claim arises; a later application may be accepted if you show
  sufficient cause (s. 45(6)).
- **What you can get:** the amount due, plus compensation of up to **ten times** the claim, depending on the
  circumstances. The authority should try to decide within three months (s. 45(2)).
- **If the employer still does not pay:** the authority sends a recovery certificate to the Collector or District
  Magistrate, who recovers it as arrears of land revenue (s. 45(3)).
- **Who is responsible:** the employer; if the employer fails, the company, firm or proprietor of the establishment
  (s. 43).
- **Appeal:** either side may appeal the authority's order within 90 days (s. 49(1)).

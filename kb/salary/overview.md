Wages are governed by the Code on Wages, 2019, which replaced the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, the Minimum Wages Act,
1948, the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 and the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 69).

"Employee" under the Code covers anyone employed on wages by an establishment to do skilled, semi-skilled, unskilled,
manual, operational, supervisory, managerial, administrative, technical or clerical work, except apprentices and
members of the Armed Forces (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 2(k)).

**When wages must be paid** (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 17(1)):
- daily workers: at the end of the shift;
- weekly: on the last working day of the week;
- fortnightly: before the end of the second day after the fortnight ends;
- **monthly: before the seventh day of the next month ends.**

**When you leave.** If you resign, or are removed, dismissed or retrenched, or the establishment closes, the wages due
to you must be paid **within two working days** (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 17(2)).

The government may set other time limits in some circumstances (s. 17(3)). Karnataka's own rules under the Code were
still in draft when LawBuddy's sources were collected, so check with the Karnataka Labour Department for procedure.

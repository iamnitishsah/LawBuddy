1. **Written demand** to HR or the owner by email, listing the unpaid months and amounts and a date to pay by.
2. **Legal notice** if there is no payment: facts, amounts, the legal deadline for payment, and a warning that you
   will file a claim. LawBuddy can draft one for you to review and sign.
3. **Wage claim** before the authority appointed under the Code on Wages (ask the Karnataka Labour Department which
   officer hears claims for your area), within three years (Code on Wages, 2019, s. 45(6)). This is usually faster and cheaper than court, and can award compensation
   of up to ten times the claim (s. 45(2)).
4. **Civil suit** as an alternative: a suit for wages must be filed within three years from when the wages became
   due (Limitation Act, 1963, Schedule, Article 7). Talk to a lawyer or the DLSA before choosing this route.

Free help: industrial workmen are among those entitled to free legal services (see "Free legal aid").
